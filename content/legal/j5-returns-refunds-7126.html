Overview

Our returns and refund policy lasts 30 days from the date of delivery. If 30 days have passed since delivery, we can't offer you a refund or exchange.

To be eligible for a return, your item must be unused, in the same condition that you received it, and in the original packaging with all instructions, documentation, tags, and accessories included. A receipt or proof of purchase is required for all returns.

All returns require a Return Merchandise Authorization (RMA). Please contact us at Info@J5Rescue.com before shipping anything back. Returns received without an RMA may be refused or delayed. Please do not send your purchase back to the manufacturer.

Non-Returnable Items

Due to the nature of the products we sell, the following items cannot be returned:

  • Body armor, ballistic panels, ballistic plates, ballistic shields, and other ballistic protection products, unless there is a manufacturing defect. Once these products leave our control, we cannot verify they have not been worn, exposed, or compromised, and we will not resell life safety equipment of uncertain condition.
  • Life safety products and hermetically sealed products, unless there is a manufacturing defect.
  • Medical supplies and sanitary or hygiene-related products that have been opened or unsealed after delivery, including but not limited to tourniquets, hemostatic agents, dressings, airway devices, and any sterile-packaged items.
  • Products with an expiration date, where the item has been opened or the remaining shelf life has been reduced while in your possession.
  • Custom, made-to-order, or personalized items, including custom-fitted armor, custom carriers, embroidered or patched apparel, medical kits, and any Goods made to your specifications.
  • Special order items ordered from a manufacturer at your request and not normally stocked by us.
  • Gift cards.
  • Downloadable or digital products.
  • Hazardous materials, flammable liquids or gases, and any items we are unable to lawfully or safely accept back by common carrier.

Items in these categories with a verified manufacturing defect will be repaired, replaced, or refunded in accordance with the manufacturer's warranty. We will assist you with the warranty claim process.

Returnable Items

Most other items we sell, such as unworn apparel and uniform items, bags and packs, tools, lights, unopened accessories, and general equipment, may be returned within 30 days of delivery subject to the conditions above.

Non-defective returns may be subject to a restocking fee of up to 15%, which will be deducted from your refund. Restocking fees are not applied to returns resulting from our error or to defective items.

Certain situations may result in a partial refund or rejection of the return:

  • Any item not in its original condition, or that is damaged or missing parts for reasons not due to our error.
  • Any item showing obvious signs of use or wear beyond opening the original packaging.
  • Any item returned more than 30 days after delivery.

Damaged or Incorrect Shipments

Please inspect your order upon delivery. If your order arrives damaged, or if you receive the wrong item or an incomplete shipment, contact us at Info@J5Rescue.com within 7 business days of delivery so we can make it right. Please keep all original packaging and, where possible, include photos of the damage; the carrier may require them for a claim. Return shipping for damaged, defective, or incorrect items is at our expense.

Refunds

Once your return is received and inspected, we will send you an email to notify you that we have received your returned item and whether your refund has been approved.

If approved, your refund will be processed to your original method of payment no later than 14 days from the day we receive the returned item. Any applicable restocking fee and non-refundable shipping charges will be deducted from the refund amount.

Late or missing refunds

If you haven't received an approved refund yet, first check your bank or card account again, then contact your card issuer or bank; there is often processing time before a refund is posted. If you've done all of this and still have not received your refund, contact us at Info@J5Rescue.com.

Sale and Closeout Items

Clearance and closeout items are final sale and cannot be returned or refunded unless defective. Items purchased at a standard promotional discount may be returned under the normal terms of this policy, with the refund based on the price actually paid.

Exchanges

We only replace items if they are defective or damaged, or if you need a different size in an eligible, unworn item. To request an exchange, email us at Info@J5Rescue.com with your order number before sending anything back.

Gifts

If the item was marked as a gift when purchased and shipped directly to you, you'll receive a gift credit for the value of your return once the returned item is received. If the item wasn't marked as a gift when purchased, or the gift giver had the order shipped to themselves to give to you later, the refund will be issued to the gift giver's original method of payment.

Agency and Purchase Order Sales

Returns on orders placed by government or law enforcement agencies, or under a written agreement or accepted purchase order, may be governed by the terms of that agreement or purchase order. Where no separate terms apply, this policy governs. Agency customers should contact their sales representative or Info@J5Rescue.com to arrange a return.

Refused or Undeliverable Shipments

Orders refused at delivery or returned to us as undeliverable will be treated as a standard return and may be subject to a restocking fee and deduction of outbound and return shipping costs. Non-returnable items refused at delivery are not eligible for a refund.
