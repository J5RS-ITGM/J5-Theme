This page explains how we ship, how long things take, and what we can and can't send to certain places. Our shipping restrictions reflect a mix of applicable law, carrier rules, manufacturer requirements, and our own company policy. In some cases we're deliberately more restrictive than the law requires, because we'd rather be careful with regulated gear. Exceptions are possible for eligible purchasers, but every exception gets reviewed and approved before anything ships.

This policy works alongside our Terms & Conditions and Returns & Refunds Policy. None of it is legal advice, and knowing the laws where you live is still up to you. If you're ever unsure whether we can ship something to your address, just ask us before you order.

Order Processing and Tracking

In-stock orders usually ship within 1 to 3 business days (Monday through Friday, excluding holidays). Made-to-order and special-order items, including some body armor, take longer, and the lead time is on the product page. If you don't see one, ask us before ordering and we'll get you a real estimate.

Orders that need age, credential, or eligibility verification won't ship until that review is done, so a quick response to any request from us keeps your order moving. If something changes and we can't ship within the timeframe we promised, we'll let you know with a new estimate and the choice to accept the delay or cancel the unshipped items for a full refund. You'll get tracking by email when your order ships. Give the carrier a few hours to show the first scan.

Shipping Methods and "Best Way"

Unless you pay for a specific service at checkout, orders ship "Best Way." That means we pick the carrier and service (usually UPS, FedEx, or USPS) based on what's in the box, where it's going, and what gets it there quickly and reliably. If you purchase an upgrade like our 2nd Day Air Upgrade, your order ships that service instead.

Two things worth knowing about expedited upgrades. First, they speed up carrier transit time only. They don't shorten processing, lead times on made-to-order items, or verification. A 2nd Day Air order that needs credential review still ships two-day service after the review clears. Second, free shipping always goes Best Way, and free shipping orders can take some extra handling time before they leave.

Ground-Only Items

We ship the following by ground service only, as a matter of J5 policy:

  • Compressed gas cylinders and CO2 cartridges, including launcher CO2 and rearming kits for CO2-activated inflatable PFDs
  • Lithium-ion batteries and products that include them
  • Defense sprays

Some of these could technically fly in certain configurations, but hazmat rules for air shipping are strict and the penalties for getting them wrong are serious, so we keep it simple and send them all ground. That means ground-only items can't ship to Alaska, Hawaii, or US territories. If your order mixes a ground-only item with an air upgrade, the shipment containing that item goes ground and we'll refund the upgrade charge for that shipment. Want the rest of your order expedited separately? Contact us before ordering and we'll set it up.

Oversized items like ballistic shields may ship freight. We'll contact you to coordinate delivery and get your okay on any extra charges before anything ships. Some products require an adult signature at the door.

Where We Ship

United States only. We don't ship internationally, and we don't ship to freight forwarders or package forwarding services. Restricted items need a physical street address, so no PO Boxes and no APO/FPO/DPO for those. Everything we sell is for end use inside the United States, and buying from us doesn't authorize export or re-export. The Export Restrictions section of our Terms & Conditions has the details.

Please double-check your shipping address at checkout. A typo can add days to a delivery and sometimes means extra carrier or reshipping charges. If you catch a mistake, contact us right away. We can usually fix an address before the label prints, but not always after.

How We Handle Restricted Items

Our checkout blocks product and destination combinations we can't fulfill, and we review orders by hand on top of that. Making it through checkout doesn't guarantee approval. If we find something we can't lawfully or won't ship, we'll cancel that item and refund you in full, right away, including any shipping charges that no longer apply. When you place an order, you're confirming you can legally buy, receive, and possess what's in it at the address it's going to.

The restrictions below apply to consumer orders. Many of them have exemptions for law enforcement, government, and military purchasers, but eligibility depends on the specific product, jurisdiction, purchaser, and purpose. Credentials alone don't automatically qualify an order, which is why agency requests get their own review (see Law Enforcement and Agency Orders below). One more thing: cities and counties sometimes go further than their state. We honor local rules where we know about them, and it's worth a quick check of your own local ordinances before you order. Not sure? Ask us and we'll help you figure it out.

Body Armor, Plates, and Shields

J5 requires body armor purchasers to be at least 18. Separately, federal law (18 U.S.C. § 931) makes it illegal for anyone convicted of a violent felony to buy or possess body armor. Our consumer shipping restrictions:

  • Connecticut: We don't ship body armor, ballistic plates, shields, or carriers containing armor to Connecticut consumers. State law there requires most armor sales to happen in person. Eligible purchasers such as agencies should contact us.
  • New York: New York limits body armor sales to people in certain eligible professions, so we don't ship armor to New York consumers without verifying eligibility first. If you qualify, sworn law enforcement for example, get in touch before ordering and we'll take care of it.
  • California: We ship armor to California, but California bars anyone prohibited from owning firearms from possessing body armor. By ordering, you're confirming that doesn't apply to you.

Note that an empty carrier and a carrier loaded with ballistic panels can have different shipping eligibility. Check the product listing, or ask us if it's not clear.

Less-Lethal

Launchers and Chemical Projectiles

CO2 launchers themselves are legal almost everywhere. The chemical rounds are where the restrictions come in. We don't ship pepper or tear gas projectiles, or kits that include them, to California, Michigan, New York, or Wisconsin. Kinetic-only launchers and kits can still go to California and New York. We don't ship any launcher products to New York City (all five boroughs), Yonkers, or San Francisco. Some other cities restrict CO2 launchers too, so check yours before ordering, and when a manufacturer's own shipping policy is tighter than ours for a specific product, we follow theirs.

Pepper Spray

Defense sprays go ground only, to street addresses only. Under our current policy we don't ship sprays to Alaska, Hawaii, Massachusetts, Michigan, Minnesota, New York, Wisconsin, or US territories. In some of those places sprays are legal to own but the sale, formulation, or shipping rules make direct orders impractical, so this list is our shipping policy, not a claim that sprays are banned everywhere on it. Washington, D.C. orders: contact us first and we'll sort out what we can send you. A few states cap canister size (California 2.5 oz, Florida 2 oz, New Jersey 0.75 oz), and we only send compliant sizes there.

Stun Guns and TASER Devices

We don't ship electronic control devices to Hawaii, Massachusetts, or Rhode Island, or to Chicago, Philadelphia, Baltimore, or Washington, D.C. Buying one in Illinois, Michigan, or Wisconsin? Send us a copy of your FOID (IL), CPL (MI), or CCW (WI) and we'll ship once we've reviewed it. Contact us for how to submit documents securely. Certain TASER-brand devices also require a background check at the time of order.

Automatic and Assisted-Opening Knives

Assisted-opening knives aren't switchblades under federal law, since the blade is biased toward closure and you start it opening yourself. We ship those to most states. Make sure carrying one is legal where you live.

Automatic knives and OTFs are different. The Federal Switchblade Act (15 U.S.C. §§ 1241–1245) restricts putting them into interstate commerce, with only narrow exceptions, so we treat every automatic knife order as restricted. We ship them only after confirming the specific sale and shipment are permitted, which in practice usually means agency purchases and official-duty transactions. Law enforcement or military status by itself doesn't automatically qualify an order, so contact us before buying and we'll walk through it with you. Automatic knives never ship through USPS. If we can't confirm your order qualifies, we'll reach out or cancel and refund it.

Firearm Parts, Magazines, and Muzzle Devices

Magazines

These are our consumer shipping limits by destination. We won't ship a magazine over the limit to these places:

  • 10 rounds: California, Connecticut, Maryland, New York, Rhode Island, Washington, Oregon, and Washington, D.C.
  • Massachusetts: 10 rounds, and 5 for shotgun magazines
  • New Jersey: 10 rounds, and 6 for semiauto shotgun magazines
  • Hawaii: 10 rounds for any magazine that works in a handgun
  • Illinois and Vermont: 10 rounds for long guns, 15 for handguns. Some Illinois towns are stricter (Chicago, Oak Park, Aurora, and Franklin Park among them), and we ship to whichever limit is tightest for your address.
  • Colorado: 15 rounds, and 8 for detachable shotgun magazines
  • Virginia: 15 rounds
  • Delaware: 17 rounds

A few wrinkles worth knowing. Extension kits and parts that push a magazine past a limit count as the magazine itself. Connecticut requires buyer credentials (a CT carry permit, eligibility certificate, or ammunition certificate) for magazines of any size, so CT orders wait on verification. Where a jurisdiction exempts law enforcement or permit holders, Delaware CCW holders for instance, we can ship after we verify. Courts are actively reshaping magazine law, so we apply whatever rules are in force the day your order ships, and our limits may be more conservative than the law strictly requires.

Parts and Muzzle Devices

Most parts and accessories (grips, rails, sights, springs, muzzle brakes, and the like) ship anywhere without issue. Our exceptions:

  • Flash hiders: we don't ship them to Connecticut or Maryland.
  • Folding and thumbhole stocks: not to Illinois. Collapsible stocks: not to Massachusetts.
  • Rate-of-fire devices: we don't sell bump stocks, binary triggers, trigger cranks, auto sears, or any other device built to increase a firearm's rate of fire.
  • Frames and receivers: anything that's legally a firearm ships to your FFL for transfer, never to your door. Illinois restricts frames and receivers (bolt-action excepted) beyond that, and Illinois orders get individual review, with consumer sales generally unavailable.
  • We don't sell unserialized or unfinished "80%" frames or receivers at all.
  • No suppressors or other NFA items on this site. Agencies that need NFA or restricted equipment should call us.

One more layer: states with assault weapon laws (California, Connecticut, Delaware, Hawaii, Illinois, Maryland, Massachusetts, New Jersey, New York, Rhode Island, Washington, and D.C.) regulate certain parts based on what gun they end up on. Threaded barrels, barrel shrouds, and in New York and Washington even muzzle brakes and compensators can turn a legal rifle into a prohibited one. We may decline to ship a specific part to those states, so before ordering, please make sure a part is legal to install on your firearm where you live. If you're not sure, reach out and we'll help you check.
