What we do

J5 Rescue Supply holds a Type 07 FFL with a Class 2 SOT (manufacturer). Our firearms work is focused on prototyping and concept design. We are not a firearm retailer, and we don't stock firearms for sale.

That said, if you're looking for a specific firearm, we can still help. Through our distributors and industry partners, we can get pricing and lead times on most firearms from a manufacturer part number or UPC.

At a Glance

  • License: Type 07 FFL / Class 2 SOT
  • Focus: Prototyping & concept design
  • Firearm sales: Special order by MPN or UPC
  • Location: Northern Illinois
