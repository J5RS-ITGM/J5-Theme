A different kind of dealer

J5 Rescue Supply started in 2017 to bridge the gap between equipment manufacturers and the people who actually use the gear: agencies, departments, and the responders who staff them.

We saw teams making expensive equipment decisions without specialist guidance, and buying the wrong items because they didn't know what they didn't know. So we focused on four things: consultation, equipment sales, custom kit packing, and sourcing.

We sell what we use. We pack what we carry.

Our team brings combined experience across fire, EMS, and in-hospital emergency care, and two of us are Army veterans (68W combat medic and Infantry). That experience goes into every gear recommendation and every kit we build.
