Orders & Shipping

When will I receive my order?

We process orders as we receive them. Typical handling time is 1 to 2 business days, and larger, custom, or kit orders may take longer. Once your order ships, expect delivery in 3 to 5 business days depending on your distance from Illinois.

What are the shipping options?

We ship using the best method for each order, choosing the carrier that can deliver your items most efficiently. Common methods include USPS Ground Advantage, USPS Priority Mail, UPS Ground, and UPS 2nd Day Air. If you need a specific shipping method, reach out before ordering. See our Shipping Policy for full details.

Do you stock the items on your website, or dropship?

We're a stocking dealer and distributor of the products on our site, and we work hard to keep website inventory matched to what's on our shelves. Some items may ship directly from the manufacturer to get them to you faster.

Can I make changes to an order I already placed?

Yes, as long as the order hasn't been processed or shipped yet. Contact us as soon as possible and we'll do our best to accommodate the change.

Do you ship internationally?

Please contact us before placing an order from outside the United States. We do ship internationally, but we need to confirm there are no export restrictions or manufacturer agreements that prevent it before accepting the order.

What is your return policy?

You can find our full return policy on our Returns & Refunds page.

Discounts & Agencies

Do you offer military or first responder discounts?

Yes. We offer discounts to military (active, reserve, and retired), law enforcement, fire, EMS, and healthcare workers. Discounts vary by product. Apply through our Military & First Responder Discount program and, once you're verified, we'll issue your coupon code.

Do you work with agencies and departments?

Yes. We outfit agencies of every size, from single-officer orders to department-wide purchases. Agencies and departments can order through our agency portal, or request a quote and we'll put one together.

Website & Privacy

Is your website secure?

Yes. Our entire site runs over an encrypted HTTPS connection, which you can confirm by the padlock icon in your browser's address bar.
